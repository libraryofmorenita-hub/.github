# Morenita Technology

**Design-forward hardware + ML infrastructure for human connection in an analog world.**

Morenita Technology is a creative technology studio building biomedical devices, machine learning systems, and community platforms at the intersection of human wellness, clean energy, and accessible design.

We believe technology should make people feel safe, capable, and connected—not monitored, exhausted, or alone. Every product we build centers on one principle: **green by default, human by design.**

---

## What We're Building

### 🏥 **Maxine** — Recovery Companion Device
A biomedical device that puts patients back in control of their wellness journey. Maxine helps nurses, caretakers, and patients track recovery, medication administration, and health metrics with minimal friction. Beautiful hardware. Thoughtful firmware. Designed for agency, not compliance.

**Status:** Prototype complete · MVP phase Q2 2025  
**Tech:** Embedded C/C++ · CircuitPython · TypeScript · PostgreSQL  
**Led by:** Mikah Tabalanza (Biomedical Engineering Lead), Amelia Arabe (Engineering)

---

### 🧠 **Morenita Signals** — ML for Creatives
A recommendation engine built differently. Instead of extracting attention, Signals amplifies human intuition. It learns what creatives *actually* care about through human-in-the-loop decision-making. Every recommendation is debuggable. Every prediction is explainable.

Powers personalized content curation across the Morenita ecosystem—from Library of Morenita courses to Muses community discovery.

**Status:** Algorithm architecture complete · Beta Q1 2025  
**Tech:** Python · FastAPI · Machine Learning · Collaborative Filtering  
**Led by:** Amelia Arabe

---

### 🎨 **Muses of Morenita** — Creative Community Platform
A judgment-free space where creatives co-create with our technology. Muses is where you test Signals, share your work, connect with other creators, and help us build the tools you actually want.

Live demos. Feedback loops. No algorithm trying to make you scroll.

**Status:** Pre-launch · Public beta Q1 2025  
**Tech:** React · Supabase · TypeScript · Real-time collaboration  
**Community Directors:** Madeline Abiera (Creative Director, Library of Morenita), Vega Robinson (Lead 3D Artist & Visual Designer)

---

### 📚 **Library of Morenita** — Education Platform
A freemium education platform rooted in community wellness, creative development, and mindful technology. Free to read and explore. Paid access to courses and full academic majors.

Home to all Morenita products, community, and the education mission that funds everything long-term.

**Status:** Pre-launch   
**Tech:** Supabase · Next.js · Stripe (payments) · PostgreSQL

## Our Philosophy

**Green by Default**
Every product incorporates renewable energy from the start—not as an afterthought. Maxine explores thermoelectric harvesting. Our hardware cases explore solar integration. This isn't marketing. It's fundamental.

**Human in the Loop**
Algorithms should amplify human intuition, not replace it. Signals is built for explainability. Muses is built for feedback. We believe the best decisions are collaborative.

**Design Meets Engineering**
We don't build products *for* people. We build infrastructure *with* people. That means our biomedical devices are beautiful. Our platforms respect your time. Our community is a safe space to explore and create.

**Accessibility First**
Technology that isn't accessible is technology that excludes. Every Morenita product is designed for the full spectrum of human ability.
